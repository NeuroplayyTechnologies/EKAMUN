WHAT IS NEXUS?

NEXUS 2026 is a student-led MUN and leadership summit by Rainbow School, bringing together young minds to debate, negotiate, research, and think beyond the classroom.

  • Debate meaningful issues and research complex agendas
  • Negotiate with different perspectives
  • Build public speaking and leadership skills
  • Collaborate with other delegates
  • Explore real-world challenges through simulations
  • Express ideas with confidence and responsibility

An experience where ideas meet action, voices meet perspectives, and delegates find their NEXUS.

where ideas meet impact

Rainbow School × EKAMUN

Rainbow School presents NEXUS 2026 in association with EKAMUN — an experience built on dialogue, diplomacy, research, leadership and critical thinking.

About Rainbow School

Established in 1975, Rainbow School, Saharanpur began with a small nursery classroom and a big dream. Founded by Mrs. Nargis Malik, it has grown into a CBSE affiliated 10 + 2 co-educational institution. Under the leadership of Mrs. Amita Malik, School Director, and Mrs. Ashu Rathor, Principal, it prepares students to think independently, communicate confidently and lead responsibly.

About EKAMUN and the collaboration

EKAMUN is a platform built around meaningful discourse, diplomacy, and the art of informed dialogue. EKAMUN × NEXUS unites two platforms around the power of dialogue, leadership and intellectual curiosity, giving young voices a meaningful space to be heard.

General Guidelines
