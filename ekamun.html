About EKAMUN

EKAMUN is a platform built around meaningful discourse, diplomacy, and the art of informed dialogue. With a strong emphasis on simulation, collaboration, and real-world engagement, EKAMUN strives to create experiences that challenge students to think critically, communicate effectively, and approach global issues with perspective and responsibility.

“Diplomacy is not simply about speaking well; it is about listening, understanding, and finding common ground.”
Chaitanya Chouhan, Founder & President, EKAMUN
